# Supplied scenario boundaries

Contribution = selected revenue - selected variable costs. Net profit is always
unknown in this narrow profile. Cash movement is a separate receipts - payments
calculation, never whole-farm cash flow. Exact multipliers produce counterfactual
sensitivity, not probability/forecast. Keep currency/basis/period/version/source/
time/state, missing costs and declared coverage. No FX, valuation or allocation.
Explicit zero stays numeric; missing/suppressed stays null. Incompatible or future
rows are retained and excluded. Known subsets never establish complete scope.
See [the portable helper contract](../../../docs/workflows/business-scenario-helper.md).
