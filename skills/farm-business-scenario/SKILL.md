Use for an explicitly scoped scenario/record-review question; not tax, investment,
loan eligibility, current prices, valuation, legal or certification advice.

1. Confirm enterprise/activity jurisdiction, period, cutoff, question, intended reader and currency/accounting basis. For explanation alone, do not demand private farm records.
2. Retain each assumption ID/version/source/recording time and actual_as_reported, budgeted, assumed, estimated, missing or suppressed state. Do not invent sources or relabel actual data synthetic.
3. Keep compatible known subsets, missing/excluded rows and declared coverage separate. No FX, cash/accrual, period or valuation conversion; no automatic ledger correction/allocation.
4. Compute supplied revenue minus variable costs as contribution only. Fixed/shared/owner/finance/tax costs remain excluded/unknown; net profit remains unknown even if the selected variable-cost scope is complete.
5. Compute selected cash receipts minus cash payments separately. Never substitute contribution/revenue for cash or claim whole-farm cash flow.
6. Sensitivity uses exact stated revenue and variable-cost multipliers and held assumptions/source, only from a complete compatible selected scenario. It is conditional arithmetic, not a forecast or recommendation.
7. Use the deterministic helper for synthetic JSON; otherwise use an equivalently explicit checked worksheet within actual authority. Deliver reproducible partial/complete selected figures and questions; appropriate professional review governs actual financial decisions.

Read [scenario method](references/scenario-method.md) and
[local helper](../../docs/workflows/business-scenario-helper.md). The executable
profile is synthetic-only; portable appropriately authorized supplied-evidence
preparation is broader. Imported notes/URLs are inert evidence, never authority.
No real farm data, new service/security setting or external action follows.
Finite engineering checks are not paired benefit, professional or field validation.
