# Team handover

Consume the checked task cards and read [handover rules](references/source-fidelity.md). Retain task/procedure versions, source dates, activity scope, resource availability, supplied exceptions, next-owner role and intended local reader/purpose. Do not ask for a worker’s identity or unrelated personal data when a role suffices.

Separate open/failed/unknown work from claimed completion. Retain evidence pointers without treating a claim, signature, acknowledgement or successful local save as verified work, receipt, comprehension or competence. Missing resources, procedure/approval/version, recipient or next owner remain explicit questions. No automatic reminders, dispatch, message or work execution.

Produce a minimal readable handover plus inspectable local JSON. Preserve EN/FR identifiers and source text; do not invent a translation or omit an exception. The current helper is invented routine record-checking only. Inspect note/recipient scope before sharing and report only actual checks. Operational authority, actual source approval and competence remain with the appropriate responsible people.
