# Weather evidence checks

Record source ID, kind, location/coverage, value state/unit/quality, issue or
observation time, valid interval and retrieval time separately. Unknown is not no
weather risk. Required numeric thresholds and freshness limits belong to the
identified source/procedure/task; the synthetic example's age is a simulation input.

ECCC [Citypage documentation](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/msc-data/citypage-weather/readme_citypageweather-datamart_en/)
was checked 2026-10-17: dated XML emission/hour fields and en/fr file variants are
documented, with separately linked tags/schema. Emission naming does not establish
farm representativeness or every forecast's valid interval. No provider payload,
icons, systematic retrieval or schema conformance is included in this helper.

The synthetic lab retains stale/revised/unknown states. Future planning from an
observation requires a forecast/other justified evidence rather than extrapolation.
Alerts are unassessed by the calendar helper; inspect applicable official warnings
and actual field/procedure conditions before any operational decision.
