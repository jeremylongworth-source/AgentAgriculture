---
name: farm-adviser-package
description: Prepare a compact local adviser handoff from verified farm records, checked results and unresolved questions. Use for adviser packages or dossier pour le conseiller; it prepares files and does not send them or claim professional approval.
---

Start with the operator's question and a verified committed record snapshot. If
records are not reconciled, obtain that evidence before quoting checked totals.
Use only the selected task scope; do not assemble an unrelated farm dossier.

Read the chosen [English export guide](../../templates/en/export.md) or
[French export guide](../../templates/fr/export.md). Run `verify`, then `handoff`
to a new local folder. Inspect its index, file hashes, source-package status and
omissions. The minimal copy contains the question-focused summary, source versus
interpretation table, calculations and exceptions. Raw CSVs, full normalized JSON,
task permissions and reviewer history remain with the farmer unless separately
selected and authorized.

Use the language's result template to explain checked values with source/calculation
references, assumptions, excluded rows and open questions. Keep incomplete scope
visible. Do not infer tax treatment, profit, eligibility, diagnosis, professional
review or adviser acceptance from a well-formed package.

Provide the local copy and its intended recipient scope for inspection. Check free
text for unnecessary personal/financial information. If the adviser needs original
evidence, identify the exact missing pieces and obtain the applicable sharing
authority; do not silently add the full source archive.

The task authorizes local preparation when already requested. External transmission
needs its own authority and confirmed outcome; stop at the local deliverable unless
that authority exists. A prepared copy is neither sent, received nor approved.
