# Livestock review handoff

Consume the checked event/feed review. Read [handoff rules](references/record-rules.md). Preserve enterprise, jurisdiction, UTC date period, cutoff, species/group/lot context, units/bases, selected IDs, correction lineage and supplied coverage. Separate deterministic arithmetic from physical source meaning and professional interpretation.

Prepare a local readable brief and inspectable JSON: what was selected, what the records support, missing/stale/conflicting information, questions for the record owner and, if requested, the appropriate veterinarian/nutrition adviser. Omit free health/person notes from the compact view; retain original notes in the canonical local JSON and inspect recipient/purpose before sharing. Do not transmit anything.

Verify values and unresolved states in EN/FR; keep identifiers and quantities literal. Report only the checks actually performed. Neither successful parsing, balanced head counts nor a stock balance is professional, welfare, nutrition, field or farmer-benefit validation.
