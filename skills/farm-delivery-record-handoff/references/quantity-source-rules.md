# Quantity/source rules

Preserve lot identity, original gross/tare values/states/units, source, observation
and recording time and correction source/reason. Use only exact kg/g/lb pairs in
the first synthetic helper; keep unsupported counts/volume/unknown units explicit.
Net mass is arithmetic gross less tare, not proof of physical accuracy or compliant
measurement. Retain future and forked revisions without using them as current.

Enterprise balances exclude explicit within-enterprise storage transfers. Coverage
is declared, never physically verified. Closing age uses observation time. Delivery
comparison concerns selected dispatch records and a supplied exact commitment
version, not a shipment/acceptance/contract verdict. Missing sources, conflicting
units/locations/allocations and partial evidence remain questions for review.
See [the portable helper contract](../../../docs/workflows/harvest-delivery-helper.md).
