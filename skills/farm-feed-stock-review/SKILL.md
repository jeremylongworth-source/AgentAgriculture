# Feed stock review

Identify the exact feed lot and review interval; preserve the source quantity unit and as-fed/dry-matter basis on every line. Read [stock rules](references/record-rules.md). Never add different lots, units or bases, convert moisture, estimate missing quantities or infer a feeding rate.

Use the checked helper for invented normalized evidence. Opening plus selected receipts minus selected removals is a listed-record balance; it becomes an expected closing under declared-complete coverage only when identity, roles, current revisions, dates, measured values, units and bases are compatible. A closing difference is unresolved, not proof of waste, consumption, theft or scale error. Coverage is supplied, not physically verified.

Return the selected IDs/sources, quantities/bases, listed and coverage-qualified balances, closing age, retained corrections and precise missing-data/mismatch questions. Suppressed, trace, estimated, missing and measured zero remain distinct. A numeric stock balance cannot establish days of safe feeding, animal needs, diet, nutritional adequacy or feed safety.
