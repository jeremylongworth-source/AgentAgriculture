# Supplied-procedure fidelity and handover

Use the [local helper contract and source boundaries](../../../docs/workflows/resource-team-helper.md).
The current normalized helper accepts routine_record_check only: source-linked desk
review steps, never machinery/clinical/safety/emergency operating instructions.

Exact ID/version/language/step order and text are data to preserve. Approval status,
source pointers, effective/review dates, enterprise/jurisdiction/asset/role and
translation-fidelity references are supplied claims, not verified authority. Missing
or mismatched context withholds quoted steps instead of generating replacements.
Available resources are also a supplied claim. Acknowledgement is not receipt proof
or competence; completion remains claimed even with an evidence reference. No actual
source approvals, completion, receipt, translation fidelity or competence are verified
by these helpers. Keep all originals and inspect sharing scope; do not send anything.
